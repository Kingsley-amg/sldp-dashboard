Statistical analysis of the programme objectives, using the same methods as the SLDP monitoring and analysis report. Models are fitted on all pilot data at every update, so the site and period filters do not apply here. A model appears once there is enough data to fit it reliably.

How this dashboard works

Every six hours an automated job downloads the three KoboToolbox forms, runs the cleaning, validation and linkage steps of the SLDP analysis pipeline, fits the statistical models and publishes the results here. Only aggregate counts and model results are published. No names, telephone numbers, Patient IDs, Event IDs or free-text notes leave the pipeline, and a final check blocks publication if any identifier pattern appears.

Eligible
Patient with confirmed P. falciparum malaria whose screening in Form 1 found no exclusion (age under 12 months, pregnancy, breastfeeding an infant under 1, severe anaemia, primaquine allergy, history of haemolysis or other exclusion).
SLDP coverage
Eligible patients given SLDP divided by all eligible patients. Status: green 90% or more, amber 75 to 89%, red below 75%.
Directly observed
SLDP swallowed in front of the health worker, as recorded in Form 1.
Dose within range
Dose in mg divided by weight in kg between 0.15 and 0.4 mg/kg (target 0.25 mg/kg, maximum 15 mg).
CEM enrolment
Patients at CEM sites who consented and were enrolled in cohort event monitoring.
Visit status
Completed: a completed Form 2 of that visit type. Due now: due date reached and still inside the grace window (Day 0: 1 day, Day 3: 2, Day 7: 3, Day 14: 3, Day 28: 5). Overdue: past the grace window without a completed visit. Not yet due: due date in the future. Exited: due after the patient withdrew, was lost or died. No baseline date: enrolled patient with neither an SLDP date nor a manual baseline.
On time
Completed visit no later than the end of its grace window.
Follow-up completion status
Green 90% or more of due visits completed, amber 75 to 89%, red below 75%.
Adverse event
A distinct Event ID reported in Form 3, or an adverse event recorded in Form 2. Serious: meets any SAE criterion (death, life-threatening, hospitalisation, disability, congenital anomaly, other medically important).
Reporting timeliness
First Form 3 report within 2 days of event onset.
Haemoglobin alert
Fall of 2 g/dL or more from Day 0 to Day 3.
Data issue severity
Critical: wrong clinical data or a broken link between forms. Major: likely error that changes an indicator. Minor: worth checking, does not change results.
Confidence intervals
Exact (Clopper-Pearson) 95% intervals for proportions; Wald intervals for model estimates.
