    Reports by facility

    Protocol objectives addressed
    1. Determine the incidence and severity of adverse events following SLDP use.
    2. Measure the change in haemoglobin after treatment (Day 0 to Day 3) and the proportion with a fall of 2 g/dL or more.
    3. Assess safety in subgroups: children, patients anaemic at baseline and those with possible G6PD deficiency.
    4. Identify risk factors for adverse events and for a fall in haemoglobin.

    The analysis covers the whole pilot and all sites; the filters do not apply here. Each test runs automatically once there are enough records and is re-run at every update. Causality of individual events is assessed separately by the FDA Technical Advisory Committee (WHO-UMC scale).

    Data and updates

    Source
    KoboToolbox web forms: Form 1 Patient Register, Form 2 CEM Form (Days 0, 3, 7, 14, 28 and unscheduled), Form 3 ADR/SAE Report for patients not enrolled in CEM. Data are pulled through the Kobo API every hour by a GitHub Action, cleaned in R and published as aggregates.
    Training records
    Patients whose first name starts with TEST, and all their Form 2 and Form 3 records, are excluded.
    Privacy
    The public page holds counts only: no names, phone numbers, Patient IDs or Event IDs. The build stops if an identifier pattern is found. The patient queue is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the queue password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 310,000 iterations) and can only be read with that password.
    Filters
    Period filters use the week of registration (enrolment and cascade), the week of Day 0 (follow-up), the week of onset (events) and the week of the call (call outcomes). Weeks start on Monday.

    Definitions

    Eligible for SLDP
    P. falciparum confirmed by test; aged 1 year or more; not pregnant or breastfeeding an infant under 1; no allergy to primaquine; no severe anaemia, dark urine or jaundice.
    CEM participant
    Given primaquine at a CEM site, enrolled in Form 1 and consenting at Day 0 in Form 2.
    Adverse event
    Any untoward medical occurrence after primaquine, recorded once per Event ID with later updates. Serious: death, life-threatening, hospitalisation or prolonged hospitalisation, disability, congenital anomaly or other medically important event.
    Signs of haemolysis
    Dark urine, jaundice or a fall in Hb of 2 g/dL or more between Day 0 and Day 3 (also pallor, fatigue and hospitalisation are tracked).
    Missed Day 3 visit
    Recorded in Form 2 with "Did the patient come for the Day 3 visit? No", the reason and a new date. It counts as an unsuccessful attempt, like an unanswered call.
    Inadvertent exposure
    Primaquine given to someone who should not have had it (pregnant, breastfeeding a baby under 1, a baby under 1, or allergic). Reported in Form 3, or in Form 2 when a CEM participant finds out she is pregnant.
    Lost to follow-up
    Two unsuccessful scheduled follow-ups (protocol). The queue lists participants who meet this definition so the team can confirm and record the disposition.
    Reporting deadlines
    FDA: serious ADRs within 7 days of onset, other ADRs within 28 days.
    Pilot targets
    SLDP coverage above 80% of P. falciparum cases; serious adverse event rate below 0.5% of CEM participants; pre-screening compliance 100% (everyone given SLDP passed the eligibility screen); DOT compliance above 95%; ADR reporting 100% (every adverse event reported to the CEM focal person or through Form 3); CEM enrolment of 1,500 with a completed Day 0 visit and 1,500 with a Day 3 full blood count; consenting 100% (patients enrolled for CEM in Form 1 who consented at Day 0). Status: Met; Close (within 10 percentage points of an "above" target, 95% or more of a 100% target, or under twice a "below" target); Not met. Enrolment: On track at 90% or more of the number expected by today, Close at 70% or more, otherwise Behind.
    Scorecard thresholds
    Green on target, amber below target, red needs action. Coverage of eligible patients: 90% / 75%. Follow-up completion: 90% / 75%. Enrolment against expected: 90% / 70%. Reporting on time: 95% / 80%.

    Statistical methods

    Proportions
    Exact (Clopper-Pearson) 95% confidence intervals. Incidence rates per 1,000 person-days with exact Poisson intervals; person-time runs from Day 0 to the last contact, exit or Day 28.
    Haemoglobin
    Paired t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test for the Day 0 to Day 3 change; multiple linear regression for predictors of the change.
    Subgroups and risk factors
    Chi-square or Fisher's exact tests; risk ratios with log-method intervals; multivariable logistic regression (odds ratios) and Cox regression (hazard ratios); Kaplan-Meier curves for time to first event and retention.
    Signal detection
    Proportional reporting ratio across all events, comparing events where primaquine is suspected with events where it is not (signal: PRR 2 or more, chi-square 4 or more, 3 or more cases). Signals are hypotheses for the safety team, not proof of causation.
